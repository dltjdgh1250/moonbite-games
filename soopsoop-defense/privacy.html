SoopSoop Defense – Privacy Policy

Developer: Moonbite Games · Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game SoopSoop Defense (package com.moonbitegames.soopsoop) handles information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no in-app purchases in the current version.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example level, items, language and sound settings) are saved only on your own device using the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device through the game, and we cannot access it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. If you have Android device backup turned on, Android may include this app data in your own Google backup under Google's terms; we do not receive it.

3. Permissions

The app declares the standard Internet permission required by its app framework. The current version does not send data over the network. The only network action is opening this policy in your browser when you tap the link in Settings.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

5. Future changes

If a future version adds advertising, in-app purchases, or any feature that processes personal data, we will update this policy and the app's store disclosures before that version is released. The effective date above will change when the policy is updated.
